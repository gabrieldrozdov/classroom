final review for unit 9! 🏆

before we begin, why are we doing this? ❓

let’s have a discussion!

  1. 1
    what’s the point of critique?
  2. 2
    what kinds of things can (or should) we critique?
  3. 3

be objective 👀

describe form ⭕️

  • →
    typography
  • →
    color
  • →
    composition (arrangement, scale)
  • →
    rhythm (energy)
  • →
    density (positive and negative space)
  • →
    image treatments (filters, framing, orientation)
  • →
    texture
  • →
    dimensionality (depth)
  • →
    ratio of type vs. image
  • →
    legibility

describe content 🔤

  • →
    subject matter
  • →
    tone
  • →
    images
  • →
    writing style
  • →
    annotation style
  • →
    reading experience and flow
  • →
    depth (of information)
  • →
    audience

be subjective 🎨

analyze something 🧪

  • →
    what’s the first thing you think of when you see each poster?
  • →
    what do the posters mean?
  • →
    who are the posters speaking to?
  • →
    what’s the most important part of each poster?
  • →
    what emotions do you feel when you look at each poster?

give your opinion 💬

  • →
    which poster do you like better?
  • →
    how long would you want to spend looking at each poster?
  • →
    what’s your favorite part of each poster?
  • →
    which part do you find least interesting?
  • →
    is the poster achieving what the artist statement says?

suggest possibilities 🔮

  • →
    what could you add or remove?
  • →
    where else could you display this poster?
  • →
    what could you change about each poster’s design?
  • →
    what could a third poster be?

let’s determine crit order! 🏁

you will be democratically deciding the order you’ll critique in.

line yourselves up in the order you want to present.

why should you go earlier? 🐇

  • →
    you want a fresh read on your work, without too much comparison to other student projects.
  • →
    you’re anxious about crit and want to get it over with.
  • →
    you’re feeling high energy and ready to go.
  • →
    you think your work could raise the energy of the room.

why should you go later? 🐢

  • →
    you want your peers to have some experience with crit before giving you feedback.
  • →
    you want deeper feedback that hasn’t already been given.
  • →
    you want to see how other crits go so that you can optimize your feedback.
  • →
    you’re feeling a little sleepy and need some time to build up energy.

once you’ve lined yourselves up, i’ll write down the crit order.

a note on taking notes 📝

it’s difficult to take notes while getting critiqued. so, everyone will be assigned a note taker. everyone will be a note taker at least once today!

your note taker will be the person that is 6 slots after your in the crit order. so, if you’re going first, your note taker will be going seventh. if there is no one 6 slots ahead of you, we’ll wrap back to the beginning.

we’ll identify the note taker right before each crit.
