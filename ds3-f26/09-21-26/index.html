small-group reviews 👀

this week, we’re going to split the class in half! one half will be in reviews, and the other will be working.

what are we reviewing? 💭

first: what question did you ask during the walkabout?

then, let’s talk through the following questions:

  1. 1
    analyze (posters)
    • →
      how are your posters being read?
    • →
      what impact does the order, number of images, annotations, and overall design have on the narrative?
    • →
      is the curatorial voice understandable and clear?
    • →
      how do your chosen techniques and other factors impact the read?
  2. 2
    analyze (public proposal)
    • →
      how do your proposed location and form engage your audience(s)?
    • →
      how will they interact with your posters?
    • →
      what would you like them to think, feel, and do?
  3. 3
    adjust
    • →
      do your collection(s) and/or curatorial voice need to be reorganized?
    • →
      are there new developments, adjustments, refinements?

iteration + documentation exercise 🔄

take your current poster design and create the following variations of both of your posters:

typographic variations 🔤

  1. 1
    the same exact posters, but with new fonts.
  2. 2
    the same exact posters, but without any text.
  3. 3
    the same exact posters, but only the text.
  4. 4
    the same exact posters, but redesign or rearrange the text (resize, rotate, reformat as needed).

compositional variations 🏗️

  1. 5
    the same exact posters, but using new colors.
  2. 6
    the same exact posters, but inverted. don’t literally invert the photos ~ if the poster uses a dark palette, make it light. if it uses a light palette, make it dark.
  3. 7
    the same exact elements, but placed differently (no redesigning, resizing, or rotating elements). you’re allowed to move the text, too.
  4. 8
    half of the elements removed from each poster.

document your work! 💾

whenever you finish a poster (or create something interesting), add it to the project’s figjam board↗.
