### 🦖 today’s exercise: how to draw an animal

this is a classic computer science exercise that’s even more relevant in the age of ai.

it’s about **giving instructions**.

#### step 1

line up based on how awake you feel.

split into groups of 2 or 3.

#### step 2

in your group, pick the first animal you see from the [random animal generator](https://www.randomlists.com/random-animals)

#### step 3

draw your animal. as you draw it, write detailed instructions for how to replicate your drawing.

**you may NOT use the animal’s name or refer to any body parts of the animal!**

**your instructions must be entirely text-based! no drawn instructions allowed!**

*you have 15 minutes.*

#### step 4

pass your instructions to the group to your left.

*spend 5 minutes drawing the animal using the instructions.*

#### step 5

once again, pass your instructions to the group to your left.

*spend 5 minutes drawing the animal using the instructions.*

#### step 6

one more time, pass your instructions to the group to your left.

*spend 5 minutes drawing the animal using the instructions.*

#### step 7

everyone pin everything up. let’s discuss: ***what techniques produced an accurate result?***

#### in conclusion

when you’re coding, you need **specific**. this was true for html and css but is *especially* true for javascript and ai.

this exercise represented what coding javascript is like. using just a basic set of principle ideas, how do you create complex things?

with ai prompting, it’s a bit less intimidating. but a new challenge arises: if you aren’t specific enough, **ai will make decisions for you!**
