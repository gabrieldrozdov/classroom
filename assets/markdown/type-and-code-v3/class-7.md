### 🤗 get comfortable!

this is your crit partner for the day. i dunno if you’re friends or not, but you’ll need to be comfy to be productive. let’s do a little warm-up exercise:

*this will take ~30min to complete.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 1}

write the letters ABCDEFGHIJKLM on a piece of paper using your normal handwriting.

*take 1 minute to write the letters.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 2}

swap papers with your teammate.

write the remaining letters NOPQRSTUVWXYZ to finish the alphabet.

match the style of your teammate’s handwriting as closely as possible.

*take 5 minutes to finish the alphabet.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 3}

swap papers back.

annotate the letters NOPQRSTUVWXYZ:

- mark letters that are your favorite, and explain why.
- mark letters that don’t fit the original style, and explain why.

*take 3 minutes to annotate the letters.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 4}

now as a team, look at both your alphabets.

explain your feedback to each other!

*take 5 minutes to share feedback.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 5}

using your feedback, draw a *new* hand-drawn alphabet A–Z that incorporates both of your styles.

*take 10 minutes to finish your design.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 6}

share your new alphabet with another team. explain your process and how you ended up at your final design.

*take 3 minutes per team (6 minutes total) to share.*

## 👀 crit p1–3!

:::

[slide]

you just simulated the process our crit will take!

your team is a unit. as individuals, you bring unique perspectives to the table. combined, you are able to achieve greater depth.

our crit is going to move like this: starting off small, and then expanding to the full class.

[slide]

**note ~** crit is NOT assessment! independently of today’s class, i will be reviewing your work and giving you a grade. today is about sharing your work with your peers, learning with each other, and getting excited about the possibilities you can keep exploring!

*this will take ~45min.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 1}

in your teams, look at your teammate’s submissions for p1–3.

you’ll be working independently for now.

for each project, **write down** the following:

1. **describe what you see ~** can you tell what the project is trying to do? do you understand it?
2. **think about new possibilities ~** how could the person expand on this project if they kept working on it?
3. **come up with questions ~** come up with 3 questions for each project (9 questions total). this could be about how the project was made. or, what other ideas the person considered while working on it.

*take 10 minutes to write all this down.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 2}

come back together as a team. discuss what you wrote down for each other.

have a conversation ~ listen carefully, and ask follow-up questions if any come up!

*take 7.5 minutes per person (15 minutes total) to share.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 3}

join together with the same team you chatted with in the first exercise.

share your projects with the other team. except, don’t talk about your own work ~ **talk about your teammate’s work based on what you just learned!**

once you share all the projects for your teammate, have a group discussion about the work. **focus on what you found most exciting/inspiring about the projects!**

*take 5 minutes per person (20 minutes total) to share.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 4}

as a group, collectively come up with 5 lessons you learned from these projects that you will remember the most.

*take 5 minutes to complete this task.*

[slide]

#### {h:primary step 5}

now let’s come back together as a class. share what you learned!
