### 🎨 consider the visual system

how can your discrete visualizations begin speaking the same visual language? how can parameters for how typography, color, and image application and treatment create a unified experience? begin exploring a consistent visual language to use by defining your system’s rules.  
- **method suggestion #1:** individually, determine rules for a visual system and explore it by applying it to your visualizations. then come back together with your team for discussion on potential combinations or one to explore further.
- **method suggestion #2:** as a team, define a set of rules. then individually explore how the system can be applied to your visualizations. come back together as a team for discussion on how you might modify or rework the system. 

### 📊 refine your visualizations!

**refine the 5+ visualizations**, now beginning to integrate a consistent visual language.
- clarify the **rules of your visual system**, and be consistent in their application.
- there should be rules for how **color** and **typography** are applied, and for how **images** and **visuals** are rendered.
